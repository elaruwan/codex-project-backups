Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

Codex Project Backups is a personal backup tool operated by Dominic Ritchie for his own use.

Google Drive access

The tool requests the drive.file permission to create and manage its own backup files in the authorised Google Drive account. It does not request access to unrelated Drive files.

Data use and storage

Project files and backup metadata are processed on the operator's own systems. Backup payloads are encrypted before being uploaded to Google Drive.

Google account data is used only to operate the backup and restore functions. It is not sold, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.

OAuth credentials are stored privately on the backup coordinator and are not published on this website.

Retention and deletion

Backups are retained according to the operator's configured retention policy. The operator can delete backups from Google Drive and revoke the tool's access through Google Account settings.
